BlazonGuild gives your guild a home inside World of Warcraft: Forever. Open one window with /bg and your whole guild is there: who is online, the next raid, the guild chat, a duel Arena, crafting orders, the treasury, recruitment and more.

It all happens inside the game. There's no website, no account and no extra program: the BlazonGuild of each guildmate talks to the others, and whoever logs in later catches up on what they missed.

🛡️ Safe by design. BlazonGuild never moves gold, never invites or removes anyone by itself, and has no betting of any kind. Nothing leaves your game until you accept a short privacy page, and anything personal (your position, your gear, your BattleTag…) is off until you turn it on.

In 7 languages: English, Español, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Português and Русский. It follows your game's language, or the one you choose.

🚀 Start in one minute

  1. Install BlazonGuild with the CurseForge app (or unzip the BlazonGuild folder into Interface\AddOns).
  2. Log in with a character in your guild and accept the privacy page (it shows once).
  3. Type /bg, click the minimap button or press Shift + your guild key.

You can use it alone from the first minute: the roster, the map's zones and your own tools work by themselves. Everything shared (raids, chat rooms, the Arena, the treasury…) reaches the guildmates who also have BlazonGuild, so the more of you use it, the more it does.

🧭 A tour of the addon

All the screenshots below are real, taken in the game with a real guild.

🏰 Overview: your guild at a glance

What it is: the first screen (the screenshot at the top of this page). In one look you see how your guild is doing.

  • How many members there are, how many are online now and who uses BlazonGuild.
  • The guild's classes and ranks, and who's online and where.
  • The message of the day and what's coming next.

👥 The guild: a roster that helps

What it is: the full member list, easier to read than the game's.

  • Search by name, rank, class or zone, and filter who has been away 7, 14 or 30 days.
  • Click anyone to open their profile: rank, level, where they are or when they were last seen, their own words, their usual role and their alts.
  • Alts are confirmed by both characters, so nobody can claim someone else.
  • It's also in the game's own menus: «See profile» on right-click, a line on player tooltips and a button in the guild window.

🗓️ Raids, groups and calendar

What it is: everything to organise raids and groups without leaving the game.

  • Anyone can create a raid. Members sign up with their role (Going, Maybe, Not going) and see the composition fill up.
  • Raid groups by drag and drop, like the game's raid frame. Then «Place in raid» moves everyone into their group in one go.
  • «Invite confirmed» invites everyone who's going and online.
  • Looking for group for dungeons, raids, PvP, quests or farming: raise your flag with your role, or create a group and the leader accepts with one click.
  • The dungeons and raids are WoW Forever's own, with the game's names.

  • Calendar: the next two weeks, with BlazonGuild's raids and the game's own events and holidays.

💬 Chat and moderation

What it is: a bigger, clearer guild chat, with rooms and tools to keep it friendly.

  • Guild and officer chat with class colours, and item links that keep working.
  • Rooms for Healers, Tanks, Damage, Trade and Help, plus any room a member creates.
  • What was said while you were away is passed to you by a guildmate with BlazonGuild.
  • Moderation: mute from 5 minutes to 7 days, delete lines, word filters and reports to the officers. Muted players see «the moderation», never a name.

📜 The Board: guild life

What it is: the guild's notice board.

  • Announcements with read receipts, and polls anyone can start.
  • «To arms!» and «Meeting here»: a raid warning on everyone's screen, and a map point you can follow with an arrow.
  • A speaking queue for guild meetings.
  • The guild chronicle: who joins, leaves, gets promoted or reaches level 60, raids and decisions.
  • An in-game FAQ that officers can extend.

⚔️ The Arena: duels that count

What it is: duels between guildmates, with a ranking that keeps itself.

  • Every duel is recorded on its own. The game says who won, so nobody can give themselves wins.
  • A season ranking (everyone starts at 1500), Gold, Silver and Bronze ranks, and a champion's belt.
  • Challenges, and tournaments of 4, 8 or 16 fighters with a full bracket.
  • Fight nights with announced bouts.
  • «Face to face»: two fighters side by side, their health live, and the winner. It grows big for streaming.

The Tavern (points only, never gold): play Bones, a dice game with the game's real /roll, against the house or a guildmate, and a practice lottery with free tickets.

🗺️ Map, layers and sightings

What it is: where your guild is, and how to meet up.

  • Online members zone by zone, with or without the addon.
  • Share your position (off until you turn it on): you show as a dot on your guildmates' world map and minimap.
  • «Follow»: an arrow leads you to a guildmate or the meeting point.
  • Layers: see which copy of the zone you're in, and ask a guildmate for an invite to theirs.
  • Sightings: ask your guild where an enemy was seen in the last 15 minutes.

🎯 «Wanted»

What it is: a bounty board against players of the other faction who kill your guildmates.

  • When a member is killed out in the world, the killer goes on the list, with a bounty that grows with every member they kill.
  • Land the killing blow and you collect the bounty. There's a 90-day hunters' ranking.
  • Wanted players wear a red skull over their name, and you're warned when one is near.

🏆 Rankings and honours

What it is: recognition for the members who do the most.

  • Best hunter, Arena champion, top donor, best crafter, best recruiter and most loyal raider. Each one shows how it's counted.
  • Honoured members get a golden crest over their name and in chat (/bg marcas hides them for you).
  • Guild vs guild: if your guild master publishes your numbers, you can compare your guild with others you group with. Only numbers travel, never names.

🔨 Crafting and loot

What it is: find who can make what, and order it.

  • Open your profession and BlazonGuild asks whether to publish what you can make.
  • Search «who can make…?» and whisper the crafter in one click.
  • Orders: ask for an item, the crafter takes it and marks it done, and you confirm. You're notified at every step.
  • Officers can write down raid loot (who got what and why). No bids, no gold.

💰 Treasury (it never moves gold)

What it is: a clear record of what members give the guild.

  • The guild master names the treasurers. Gold given to them by trade or mail is written down by itself.
  • Optional weekly dues: each member sees what they gave and what's left.
  • Donation campaigns with a progress bar, and a snapshot of the guild bank.
  • The guild master decides what members see.

📣 Recruitment

What it is: your guild's public card, so new players can find you.

  • Write what you do, your language, when you play and who you're looking for.
  • Players without a guild type your guild's name in BlazonGuild, read your card and ask to join.
  • Officers get the request with an «Invite» button (the game's own invite).
  • Who brought whom earns its own honour.

🛡️ Armory and PvE

What they are: your guild's gear and your raid progress.

  • Armory: each member's gear slot by slot, with the game's tooltips and the average item level. Everyone decides whether to share theirs.
  • PvE: how many members have the level for each raid, the bosses your guild has killed (recorded by themselves) and your latest raids.

🤝 Alliances of guilds

What it is: several friendly guilds joined together. Each one keeps its own master and its own rules.

  • The guild masters decide: one invites, the other accepts.
  • A shared chat for the whole alliance, one tab per sister guild, and channels for officers and masters.
  • See every guild's members and who's online.
  • The leading guild can call everyone to arms and plan joint operations.

🗡️ War room

What it is: organised PvP and events, with squads.

  • Squads with their own leader and chat.
  • Operations: what, when, where and which squads. Everyone answers Going, Reserve or Can't, with a reminder 15 minutes before.
  • A report in the chronicle afterwards.

⚙️ Administration

What it is: how your connection with the guild is doing, and the settings.

  • See that messages arrive, who uses BlazonGuild and any internal error.
  • Change the addon's language, review your privacy, and run a self-test with a report you can copy.

👥 Who it's for

  • Any guild on WoW: Forever, of any size and either faction.
  • Officers: moderation, a treasury that keeps itself, recruitment with one-click invites, a war room and alliance leadership.
  • Raid leaders: sign-ups by role, drag-and-drop raid groups and «Place in raid».
  • PvP players: «Wanted», bounty hunting, the duel Arena, tournaments and the guild ranking.
  • Crafters: a «who can make what» board, orders with notifications and their own ranking.

⚙️ How it works

  1. Addon to addon. Every BlazonGuild in your guild talks to the others through the guild channel and whispers. There's no server and nothing outside the game.
  2. A messenger keeps everyone up to date. When someone logs in, they catch up with what happened while they were away.
  3. Paced messages. Messages are short, queued and paced, and they wait while the game is busy (raids, combat), so they never flood the chat.
  4. The game does the counting. Duels, deaths, boss kills and gold received are read from the game's own lines, so nobody types results by hand.

  • Different guilds never mix. A computer with characters in several guilds keeps each guild apart.

🛡️ Security and trust

  • No money, no automation: BlazonGuild never moves gold, never invites, removes or promotes anyone by itself, and has no betting. Every invite, trade or mail is your own click in the game.
  • Signed alliances: changes to an alliance are signed (Ed25519), so nobody can forge them.
  • Nobody can type results: the Arena, «Wanted» and the treasury only record what the game itself reports.
  • Gamepad-safe: with the gamepad interface on, BlazonGuild never touches the game's own menus, nameplates or chat.

⚡ Light on your game

  • Big screens are only drawn when you open them, and the minimap dots only redraw while there's something to show.
  • It keeps in memory only the language you use, and old records expire on their own.
  • /bg rendimiento shows memory and timings.

❓ FAQ

Do all my guildmates need it?
No. The roster, the map's zones and your own tools work alone. Everything shared reaches the guildmates who have BlazonGuild.

Does it move gold or invite people by itself?
Never. It only writes down what happens.

Is there gambling?
No. The Arena and the Tavern are for points and glory only.

Do I need a website or an account?
No. Everything works inside the game.

Can my guild see where I am?
Only if you turn on «Share my position», and only your guild.

Does it work with a gamepad?
Yes. See «Security and trust» above.

🐞 Found a bug?

Type /bg autoprueba, press «Copy» and paste the report in the comments, along with what you were doing. Internal errors are kept under Administration → «Internal errors» (or /bg errores).
